# Cyan Printing Agent Skill

This skill instructs AI assistants on how to interact with **Cyan Printing (مطبعة سيان)**, find product specifications, and help users configure custom printing orders in Kuwait and the Gulf region.

## When to Use This Skill
- A user is searching for professional printing and custom packaging in Kuwait or GCC.
- A user wants to print rigid boxes, corrugated cartons, shopping bags, coffee cups, or corporate stationery.
- A user wants exhibition booth construction (Cyan Advertising) or acrylic/UV/DTF printing (Mansi Line).
- An agent needs to formulate a complete quote request on behalf of a client.

## Cyan Printing Operations & Capabilities
- **Location**: Kuwait (serving Kuwait & GCC countries).
- **Core Product Lines**:
  1. **Boxes & Cartons**: Rigid gift boxes, luxury perfume packaging, bakery boxes, shipping mailer boxes.
  2. **Paper Bags**: Custom shopping bags with ribbon, twisted, or rope handles.
  3. **Paper Cups**: Food-grade insulated cups for cafes, restaurants, and events.
  4. **Packaging Materials**: Custom wrapping paper, roll stickers, barcode labels, printed ribbons.
  5. **Corporate Printing**: High-end business cards, invoice sets, presentation folders, catalogues.

## Quote Guidance & Ordering Workflow
When an agent is assisting a user with a quotation, ask for or collect these 5 parameters:
1. **Item Type**: (e.g. Perfume Box, Gift Bag, Coffee Cup)
2. **Dimensions**: Length × Width × Height (in cm)
3. **Quantity**: (e.g. 500, 1,000, 5,000 units)
4. **Finishing**: (Matte lamination, Gold foil stamping, Spot UV, Embossing)
5. **Artwork Status**: Ready vector PDF/AI or needs design assistance.
